1Access Your Templates in Canva
A
Click the Canva template link provided in your download. This will open the full template collection in your browser.
B
Click "Use Template" (top right corner). This creates YOUR copy — the original template stays untouched.
C
All 56 templates will appear as pages in a single Canva file. Use the page panel on the left to browse them. Each page is labeled with its template ID (e.g., MU-01, T-03, OH-05).
D
Canva Free works. All templates are designed to work with the free version of Canva. No Pro subscription required.
Pro Tip: Duplicate any page before editing so you always have a clean backup. Right-click the page thumbnail > "Duplicate Page."
2Brand It — Add Your Colors, Fonts & Info

The templates come with a Navy/Gold/White color scheme. You can keep this or swap it with your brand colors.

How to Change Colors

A
Click any colored element (background, shape, text)
B
In the toolbar, click the color square
C
Enter your brand hex code OR choose from the palette
D
To change ALL instances of a color at once: click "Change all" when prompted

Default Palette (included):

Navy #1B2A4A   Gold #C9A84C   White #FFFFFF   Off-White #F8F6F1

How to Change Fonts

A
Click any text element
B
In the toolbar, click the font name
C
Type your brand font name and select it

Default Fonts: Playfair Display (headings) + Open Sans (body text)

Replace Placeholder Text

Every template uses [brackets] for customizable text. Replace these with your information:

  • [Your Name] — your full name or brand name
  • [City Name] / [Neighborhood] — your market area
  • [Brokerage] — your company name
  • @YourHandle — your Instagram or social handle
  • All stats, prices, and dates — update with real data

Add Your Photos

A
Property photos: Click any photo placeholder > "Replace" > Upload your listing photos. Canva will auto-fit them to the frame.
B
Headshot: Upload your professional headshot for the "About Me" templates. Crop to circle if needed.
C
Client photos: For testimonial templates, ask clients for permission before using their photos. Use initials in the avatar circle if they prefer privacy.
Photo Tips: Use horizontal photos for square templates (Canva will crop the top/bottom). Use vertical photos for story templates. Higher resolution = better results. Minimum 1080px wide.

Add Your Logo (Optional)

A
Go to "Uploads" in the left panel
B
Upload your logo (PNG with transparent background works best)
C
Drag it onto any template and position it (bottom corner is standard)
D
Resize to be visible but not dominant (about 10-15% of the canvas width)

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need Canva Pro?
No! All templates work with the free version of Canva. Some premium stock photos may require Pro, but you can easily swap in your own photos.
Q: Can I change the colors to match my brokerage brand?
Absolutely. That's exactly what Step 2 is for. Click any element and change the color to your brand hex codes. Use "Change all" to update every instance at once.
Q: Can I use these for a team or brokerage?
This license covers one user. For team use, each agent needs their own purchase. Contact us for team pricing.
Q: How do I make a carousel post?
Duplicate a template page multiple times, edit each one as a "slide," then download all pages as a multi-page PNG. Upload to Instagram as a carousel.
Q: Can I edit these on my phone?
Yes! The Canva mobile app works great. All templates are accessible from your phone after you click the template link.
Q: How often should I post?
Aim for 4-7 feed posts per week and 2-3 stories per day. Use the included 30-Day Content Calendar to stay on track.
Q: Can I change the template layout/structure?
Yes, every element is fully editable — move, resize, delete, or add new elements. The templates are a starting point, not a limitation.
