### Caption 35 — Closing Costs Explained
**Template:** TE-01
```
"What are closing costs and why do I need them?" 🤔

Great question. Here's the breakdown:

Closing costs = the fees you pay to finalize your home purchase. They typically run 2-5% of the purchase price.

On a $400K home, that's $8,000-$20,000.

What's included:
→ Lender fees (origination, processing)
→ Title insurance
→ Home inspection
→ Appraisal
→ Escrow deposits (taxes + insurance)
→ Attorney fees (in some states)

Pro tip: You can often negotiate for the seller to cover some or all of your closing costs. I do this for my buyers all the time.

Save this post for when you need it. 📌
```
