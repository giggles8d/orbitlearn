## STORY STRATEGY (2-3 per day)

### Story Types to Rotate:
1. **Behind the scenes** — showings, open house prep, office life
2. **Polls & quizzes** — "True or false?", "This or that?", "Guess the price"
3. **Quick tips** — 15-second video tips on buying/selling
4. **Client wins** — closing day photos, review screenshots
5. **Personal moments** — coffee, pets, weekend activities
6. **Market updates** — quick stat graphics
7. **Q&A** — use the question sticker weekly

### Story Highlights to Create:
- **About Me** — intro + credentials
- **Buyer Tips** — saved educational stories
- **Seller Tips** — saved seller content
- **Reviews** — client testimonials
- **Listings** — current active listings
- **Sold** — closed properties
- **Market Updates** — monthly market data
