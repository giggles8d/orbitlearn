## PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

**Stop spending hours designing social media posts. Start posting like a top-producing agent in minutes.**

These 50+ fully editable Canva templates are designed specifically for real estate agents, brokers, and teams who want a polished, professional Instagram and Facebook presence without hiring a designer.

Every template is drag-and-drop ready. Just add your photos, swap in your colors, and post.

### What's Inside:

**56 CANVA TEMPLATES**
Sized for Instagram Feed (1080x1080) and Stories/Reels (1080x1920):

- 14 Market Update Templates — monthly reports, interest rates, neighborhood spotlights, weekly recaps, stat highlights, myth busters
- 12 Testimonial Templates — client quotes, 5-star reviews, Google review cards, before & after stories, closing day celebrations, agent stats
- 18 Open House & Listing Templates — just listed, just sold, open house promos, coming soon teasers, price reductions, photo grids, virtual tour covers
- 6 Tips & Education Templates — buyer tips, seller staging advice, rent vs buy comparisons, numbered lists
- 6 About Me / Branding Templates — agent intro cards, fun facts, value propositions, day in the life

**50-CAPTION SWIPE FILE**
Never stare at a blank screen again. 50 pre-written, customizable captions organized by category. Just fill in the [brackets] and post. Includes hashtag suggestions for every caption.

**30-DAY CONTENT CALENDAR**
A complete month of strategic posts mapped to templates and captions. Includes daily posting themes, story ideas, hashtag strategy, and a metrics tracker.

**3-STEP CUSTOMIZATION GUIDE**
Beginner-friendly PDF showing exactly how to access, brand, and export your templates. Includes FAQs, color/font reference, and the complete template index.
