## PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

**Finally, a budget system built for people who don't get a regular paycheck.**

If you're a freelancer, gig worker, creator, or side hustler, you know the struggle: your income changes every month, tax season is terrifying, and most budget templates assume you earn the same amount every two weeks.

**This bundle was designed around how freelancers actually earn and spend.**

### What's Inside:

**SPREADSHEET SYSTEM (Google Sheets + Excel)**
- Dashboard -- see your monthly profit, income sources, expense breakdown, savings progress, and debt status in one view
- Income Tracker -- log every payment with automatic tax set-aside calculation (25-30%)
- Expense Tracker -- categorize spending, flag recurring costs, and mark tax-deductible expenses
- Monthly Budget -- expected vs. actual columns with budget health scoring
- Tax Reserve Calculator -- quarterly estimated tax tracking with deduction checklist
- Debt Snowball Tracker -- auto-prioritizes smallest balance first with rollover payment logic
- Savings Tracker -- goal progress bars, deposit log, 52-week challenge, and $1K-in-30-days challenge

**PRINTABLE PLANNER (PDF - US Letter 8.5" x 11")**
- Monthly Budget Sheet
- Income Log
- Expense Tracker
- Savings Tracker with 52-week challenge grid
- Debt Payoff Planner with snowball strategy steps
- Financial Goal Planner with quarterly review checkpoints

**QUICK START GUIDE (PDF)**
- 2-page beginner-friendly guide
- Step-by-step setup instructions
- Explanation of every tab and formula
- Pro tips for freelancer finances

### Why Freelancers Love This:

- Built for VARIABLE income (not a 9-to-5 paycheck)
- Auto-calculates your tax set-aside so you're never caught off guard
- Debt snowball method keeps you motivated
- Works digitally AND on paper -- use whichever you prefer
- All formulas are pre-built -- just enter your numbers
- Clean, professional design you'll actually want to use

### How It Works:

1. Download your files instantly after purchase
2. Make a copy of the Google Sheet OR open the Excel file
3. Start logging your income and expenses
4. Watch your Dashboard come to life with real-time insights
5. Print the planner pages for physical tracking

### File Formats:
- Google Sheets (make a copy from the link)
- Excel (.xlsx)
- PDF printable pages (US Letter 8.5" x 11")
- PDF Quick Start Guide

**Take control of your freelance finances today. No more guessing, no more tax surprises, no more stress.**
