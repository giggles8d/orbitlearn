Debt Payoff Planner

Debt Snowball Method: Pay off the smallest balance first for quick motivation wins.

How it works: List all debts from smallest to largest balance. Pay minimums on everything. Throw all extra money at Debt #1. When it is paid off, roll that entire payment into Debt #2, creating a "snowball" effect.
